**The Relay checks its own storage every day (report-only), and the usage email shows the real total across both
relays.** Backend only — no change for users. Released prod+dev (squash `37c9d45`, tag `v7.22.0`, branch

## What shipped

One worker file, two deployments (`RELAY_ENV` prod | dev). Each relay emails a daily summary: both relays' usage and
the account Total (storage per namespace, decimal MB, "no data" never 0, key-count cross-check), plus a REPORT-ONLY
storage check — what it would delete (extra copies beyond the newest 2, interrupted saves) and what it keeps. Schedules
are dispatched by their exact text; strays do nothing and are counted in red. `/run-summary` sends one on demand.
`WORKER_VERSION` is printed in every email.

## What went right

- **Report-only first, then four days of matching the hand inventory.** Production's check found exactly the one real
  leftover (the Sep 9 interrupted save) and touched nothing; dev's tracked a day of testing and its cleanup.
- **The data explained itself.** "Why did dev show 288.9 MB two days running?" — Cloudflare's figure is the day's
  MAXIMUM; the next quiet day showed the true level (253.9 MB with zero activity). And the next day's email showed the
  7.21.1 app cleanup removing the 6 extras on the first save.
- **"Other 27" investigated, not shrugged off** (Ron: "totally on board with investigating something we don't
  understand"). A read-only key listing printed only categories — never a Channel ID — and named all 27: 11 revoked
  channels × 2, 3 first-used records, 2 alert keys. The deleting release will print an expected count per kind.
- **The economics were re-measured, not remembered.** Pricing re-checked (unchanged); storage per Ron-sized user is
  ~150–230 MB, not the doc's 40 MB. $5/mo ≈ 200 heavy users; storage is the cost that grows (~10¢/user at 1,000).
- **Merging main before release paid off:** the commit checks now run on the branch, and the merge exposed that the
  plan's "resolve in main's favor" would have dropped the branch's WORKER_VERSION rule from CLAUDE.md.

## What went wrong

- **Cloudflare's schedule incidents (10-12 → 10-14)** delayed schedule changes by hours and sent duplicate emails; a
  replaced 10-minute test schedule kept firing. Answer built in: dispatch by schedule text, strays counted in red, and
  never test with temporary schedules — `/run-summary` instead.
- **I gave Ron a wrong way to run `/run-summary`** — "open it in a browser" and then a GET command, when the route only
  answers POST. Instructions from memory, not from the route's code (Law 2). Cost one round.
- **The gate's batch fix (10-16) refused its first real re-send.** The probe had shown the first call of a message
  isn't in the transcript yet; I didn't follow that to "a re-send is always a first call". The six simulated cases
  didn't include a re-send — the very case the fix was for. Fixed today (`a89a80b`); Law 3 reprise added.
- **I wrote "redeployed to both relays" into the TODO before the deploys** — caught before the push and corrected.
  A result is written after it happens (Law 12).
- **The economics doc's storage figure was ~5× too low for two months** — nobody re-measured after the device-state
  journal and descriptions/reviews grew the payload.
