   local relay with no hand-holding (sprint 1's tooling paying off).

## What went wrong

1. **My false claim in the help notes' stamp** — "the reset entries' 'save a backup first' now has the button" — about
   entries that don't exist. Caught by checking the stamp's claims before committing (Law 12, the day after it was
   written: a header that describes something is a claim). Replaced by a real entry.
2. **A search tool pointed at the wrong folder** returned "no matches" for something certainly there (APP_VERSION) —
   caught because the empty answer was impossible; re-asked by explicit path. An impossible "nothing found" is a wrong
   question, not an answer.
