# Post-mortem — v7.25.1 (2026-11-06): paste, the hidden scrollbar, Reset with other tabs

_Released 2026-11-06: squash `ed96c7e` on main, tag `v7.25.1`, pushed to prod and dev. Branch `fix/misc-fixes` kept
(alphas 1–4). Started as three findings of the logging comb (LOGGING.md F1, F3) plus a scrollbar Ron reported; F2 stays
on `feature/logging`, which then merged main in._

## What shipped

1. **The status bar no longer covers the panes.** It floated over the window's bottom edge and the panes left a fixed
   24 px for it; with Cloud Sync's 24 px icon the bar is 33 px, so it covered the right pane's horizontal scrollbar — a
   sliver at 125% zoom, gone at 150%. Now it's a row of the layout. LAYOUT-001–007.
2. **Paste.** Ctrl+V and right-click → Paste were two hand-kept copies of one paste that knew only folders: a cut
   pasted into a Book List or the Trash took the book out of its folder and put it nowhere, then said "Pasted 'X' into
   'folder'"; Ctrl+V refused the Inbox (the menu allowed it); cut-paste receipts were blank (the paste cleared the
   toast's own text). Now one paste, one "can books be pasted here?", and the Book List paste the 10-21 decisions call
   for (copy adds; cut moves; a book leaving its last folder goes to the Inbox, said in the message; one undo step).
   PASTE-001–009.
3. **Reset App with other tabs open.** No tab ever let go of the book store: the delete was blocked yet Reset said done,
   the reset tab's reload could hang, and the other tab's next save wrote the old folders back. Now one connection per
   tab, released on the browser's version-change signal; a tab told "reset in another tab" stops saving and says so (Ron's
   words); one delete for all four resets waits for a tab that won't let go. RESET-001–005.
4. **The screen-test driver grew up:** Chrome (Ron's browser), scrollbars shown, zoom and window size, second tabs,
   `show_boxes()`, `covered_scroll_boxes()`. The fixture files one book in two folders.

## What went right

1. **Ron's eyes beat my instruments.** His screenshots ended a scrollbar chase my emulation couldn't reproduce; his
   "least astonishment" question re-checked the move-into-Book-List decision; his wording fixes ("in no folder… in the
   Inbox" contradicts itself; "out of date", not "out of sync") made the messages right.
2. **Every fix started with a failing test** — and two tests caught bugs no one had reported (blank receipts; the
   folders coming back after a reset).
3. **Structure, not patches:** one paste + one answer; one connection + one delete with one source of words; the bar as
   a layout row instead of a guessed gap. Two of the three fixes removed code.
4. **Shown it can fail:** each first-run pass was mutated (wrong counts, a relay that refuses the page, the fix's lines
   removed) and seen to refuse.
5. **One state, not two flags** (Law 17, the gate's item 2): the store's `ready / deleting / reset / upgraded`.

## What went wrong

1. **A theory before a grep** (Law 2): I proposed the `thin` style as the culprit without checking anything used it.
2. **The test browser wasn't Ron's browser.** Playwright hides scrollbars in a hidden browser by default, and emulated
   zoom scales the scrollbar along with the page (real zoom doesn't). Both hid the bug. Fixed in the driver; Law 8.
3. **`chrome.exe --version` opened a window on Ron's desktop** — on Windows a GUI program doesn't print a version, it
   starts. Never launch a GUI program to ask it something.
4. **Incomplete test steps:** step 4 ("Ctrl+V in the Trash") left out that step 2's cut had emptied the clipboard. Ron
   saw "no toast". Test steps carry their own setup.
5. **Probe mistakes** cost runs: a folder row taken for a book, the left pane's accordion hiding entries, a count read
   during the load's brief "All Books (0)", the first number of "(4/22)" read as the count. A probe reads the screen
   as a person would — or it measures itself.
6. **The merge duplicated a helper** written on both branches (`console_matching`). Driver changes both branches need
   belong on main first.

## Laws touched (PRINCIPLES.md)

- **Law 19 (new, Ron):** when the screen looks wrong, make the boxes visible before theorizing; outline, not border; a
  fixed gap that guesses another element's size is a bug waiting for it to change.
- **Law 4 (widened, Ron):** after 3 iterations — or the 2nd related break — question the abstraction.
- **Law 8:** the test's browser must see what a person sees — scrollbars shown; emulated zoom isn't real zoom; when a
  person's screen shows what the test can't, ask for the picture first.
- **Law 5:** three classes found today — two copies of the paste, four deleters of the book store, four resets' lists.
