# Post-mortem — v7.21.1 (2026-10-13)

**Every relay save cleans up what it absorbed.** Released prod+dev (squash `5f4e493`, tag `v7.21.1`, branch
`fix/relay-storage-cleanup` KEPT). One alpha. Step B1 of the relay-storage plan (design §14a fix 1).

## What shipped

Reclamation moved INTO `commitGeneration` — the one step every writer passes through — so Restore (which never
reclaimed its ~18 MB reset run) gets it, and every save retries earlier leftovers from the manifest's full
`absorbedRuns`. Dedupe of `absorbedRuns` there too. The separate post-commit calls were removed.

## What went right

- **The chokepoint the design named was the right one.** Three writers, one commit function: fixing it there fixed
  Restore without touching Restore's code.
- **Proven on real storage, not just a log line.** A same-content Restore on dev printed "🧹 Reclaimed 1 absorbed bulk
  run(s)", and a read-only inventory confirmed 0 multi-part runs left (dump deleted after).
- **The morning inventory reframed the problem correctly:** dev's old Restore packages had already been reclaimed by
  Imports — so the leak is permanent only where nothing imports (prod), which is exactly what "retry on every save"
  fixes.
- **A side finding with real consequences:** Ron's console showed GoatCounter blocked by uBlock — his desktop never
  reported events, so historical "testing pollution" came mostly from the dev worker, not his browser.

## What went wrong

Nothing notable. (One gap remains by design: a channel that never *saves* keeps its leftovers until the B2 daily sweep.)
